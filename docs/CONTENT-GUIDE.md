## Voice and wording

- Confident, concrete, first person ("I design…", "I lead…"). Lead with outcomes and
  scale, not duties.
- Name what a recruiter recognises: "Singapore Government", "National Single Window",
  "global publishing and edtech leaders". Do not rely on readers knowing Wiley or Pearson.
- Avoid self-diminishing framing such as "intern → lead" in headlines. Factual job
  titles in the timeline are fine.
- Prefer real tool names (GitHub Copilot, not "Co-Pilot"; k6, Playwright).
- Plain English only; see the character rules in CLAUDE.md.
- **Impact-driven bullets:** lead with the outcome, then how. Pattern: "[Outcome] by [action]"
  or "[Outcome]: [what I did]". Use scale the reader recognises (national platform, 6
  government services, adopted by another team). Never add numbers that are not in the
  facts sheet.
