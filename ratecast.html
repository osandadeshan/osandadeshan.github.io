Highlights

  • Today's Wise mid-market rate with the change since yesterday
  • Forecasts for tomorrow and month end with an 80% likely range
  • Six forecasting models blended by their past accuracy
  • Every forecast is scored against the published rate, and the misses shape the next forecast
  • Compares what the recipient gets for 1,000 SGD across providers
  • Daily sync with Vercel Cron and Neon Postgres, all on free tiers
